I'm a roboticist and controls engineer. I spent the last four years at Commonwealth Fusion Systems leading the bolometry diagnostic for SPARC — designing sensors and cameras that have to survive strong magnetic fields, neutron radiation, and heat, and writing the tomography that turns their signals back into a picture of the plasma.

Before fusion I worked on robot learning and control: a research internship at DeepMind on making reinforcement learning policies gentler on real hardware, a simulation lead role at Vicarious Surgical, and a masters at Penn's GRASP Lab with Vijay Kumar and Mark Yim on perception-based quadrotor control and graph neural networks for swarms. Before that I did flight controls for an electric VTOL aircraft at Kitty Hawk. I studied mechanical engineering at MIT, where I ran the student makerspace and founded the combat robotics club.

The thread running through all of it is hardware that has to work in the physical world, and the software that makes it work. I care most about climate — which is how I ended up in fusion, and roughly where I'd like to stay.

I'm currently looking for my next role. If that overlaps with what you're working on, get in touch.
