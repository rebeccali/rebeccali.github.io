Experience

{% include logo.html org="cfs" %}

Commonwealth Fusion Systems

Senior Diagnostician · Diagnostician · Diagnostics Support Engineer · Devens & Somerville, MA

2022 – 2026

Led the bolometry diagnostic for SPARC end to end: physics requirements, sensor and camera design, electrical and mechanical integration, testing, and project management. Bolometers measure the power radiated out of the plasma, which means putting delicate sensors close to a tokamak — rapidly changing magnetic fields, neutron flux, and temperatures that rule out most of the obvious engineering answers. I coordinated the design across tokamak systems, CAD, supply chain, and program management, and developed the tomographic reconstruction that inverts the measurements into a picture of where the plasma is radiating. First author on the diagnostic's Review of Scientific Instruments paper. I started in 2022 as a Diagnostics Support Engineer, designing active optics and mechatronic interfaces for SPARC diagnostics.

{% include logo.html org="vicarious" %}

Vicarious Surgical

Robotics Software Engineer · Waltham, MA

2021 – 2022

Technical lead for a physics-based, photorealistic simulation of robotic surgery in ROS 2 and Gazebo, used to generate synthetic training data for machine learning where real surgical data is scarce and expensive. Alongside the simulation work I set up the team's CI and coding practices and reviewed code for more junior engineers.

DeepMind

Research Scientist Intern · London, UK (remote)

2021

Research on deep reinforcement learning policies for real robots, aimed at the gap between what works in simulation and what a physical machine can survive: reducing actuator wear-and-tear and making interaction safer, on open-source GCP infrastructure.

{% include logo.html org="grasp" %}

University of Pennsylvania, GRASP Lab

Graduate Researcher · Philadelphia, PA

2018 – 2021

Three strands of work with Vijay Kumar and Mark Yim. I designed and verified a robust, perception-based controller for a UAV — one with guarantees that hold when the state estimate is coming from onboard vision rather than motion capture (IROS 2020). I built a dynamics simulation with an aerodynamic model for a one-centimetre, single-actuator UAV, as a design tool for a vehicle too small to instrument conventionally. And I designed and implemented the machine learning pipeline applying graph neural networks to decentralized swarm control, where each robot must act on only what its neighbours can tell it (ICRA 2022).

{% include logo.html org="kittyhawk" %}

Kitty Hawk

Controls Engineer · Mechanical Engineering Intern · Palo Alto, CA

2016 – 2018

Designed and flight-tested autonomous take-off, landing, and navigation for an electric VTOL aircraft — LQR control, Kalman filtering, and online optimization for allocating thrust across actuators — and built the infrastructure for Monte Carlo simulation and flight data analysis that the team used to decide whether a change was safe to fly. As an intern the summer before, I prototyped a ballistic recovery system from ultra-light parachutes and off-the-shelf rockets, now US Patent 10,507,929.
